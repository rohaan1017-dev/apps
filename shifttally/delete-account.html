Delete your ShiftTally account

You can delete your ShiftTally account and all of its data at any time, in the app or by email.

App: ShiftTally · Developer: Rohaan Patel

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open ShiftTally and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE, then tap Delete account to confirm.

Your account and all your jobs, shift records and paychecks are deleted straight away. This can't be undone. Want a copy first? Tap Export all shifts on the Profile tab to save a CSV file.

Without the app

Email rohaan1017@icloud.com from the email address you use for ShiftTally, with the subject Delete my ShiftTally account. We may reply to that address to check the request is yours. We'll delete your account and data within [30] days and let you know when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, password (stored only as a hash), account ID and sign-in sessions.
  • All your jobs: names, colours, rates, overtime settings and pay schedules.
  • All your shift records: dates and times, breaks, hourly rates, card tips, cash tips, tip-outs and notes.
  • All the paychecks you recorded: pay periods, amounts paid and notes.

What may be kept for a limited time

  • We don't keep separate database backups on our current hosting plan, so no backup copy outlives the deletion. If that changes, this page will say how long backups are kept.
  • Our authentication provider's security logs (sign-in events and IP address) are kept for a limited time by that provider, then removed.
  • Files you exported or shared from the app yourself (for example a CSV you emailed) aren't held by us, so we can't delete them.

Only want to remove some data?

You don't have to delete your account. In the app, tap any shift and choose Delete shift, remove a recorded paycheck with Edit paycheck → Remove, or archive a job you no longer work.
