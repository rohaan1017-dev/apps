Common questions

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder. If you try to sign in before confirming, the app offers Resend email. Open the link on the same phone where you signed up.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. Open the link we send on the same phone, then choose a new password.

I forgot to clock in or out

Tap Add shift to enter a shift by hand, or tap any shift to change its job, date, times, break, rate, tips or notes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, ShiftTally treats the shift as ending the next day.

How is pay worked out?

Time worked (end minus start, minus the unpaid break) times your hourly rate. If the job has overtime turned on, hours over the job's weekly limit (40 by default, the US federal rule) in a workweek add the overtime premium, 1.5× by default. The paycheck estimate is wages, overtime and card tips; cash tips are shown separately because they usually aren't on your paycheck, and tip-outs are taken off what you keep. Some states and employers count overtime differently, so you can change or turn off overtime for each job in Profile → Jobs. These are estimates, not tax advice: your pay stub is the official record.

How do I check my paycheck?

On the Pay tab, find the pay period and tap Check my paycheck. Enter the gross pay from your pay stub (before taxes and deductions). If it's more than a dollar and 1% less than your record, ShiftTally flags it so you can ask your manager or payroll.

How do I get my data out?

Profile → Export all shifts makes a CSV file for a spreadsheet and opens your phone's share sheet, so you choose where it goes. Pay → Year → Share summary shares a short text of the year's totals for your tax records.

Can I use ShiftTally on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same email on each device and your shifts are there.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the Profile tab, tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Without the app, see Delete your account.

What data does ShiftTally keep?

Your email, your jobs, shifts and recorded paychecks, and a few sign-in security records. The privacy policy has the details.
