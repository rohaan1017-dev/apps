Know what you earned, every shift

ShiftTally is your own record of the hours you work and what you expect to be paid. Track card and cash tips, see when overtime kicks in, and check every paycheck against your record. Free, with no ads and no tracking.

  • Get it on Google Play
  • Download on the App Store

  • Check your paycheck

    See the expected paycheck for every pay period. Enter the gross pay from your pay stub, and ShiftTally flags the difference if it is less than your record, so you can ask your manager or payroll.

  • Tips, done properly

    Card tips and cash tips tracked separately, plus the tip-outs you share. Cash tips stay out of the paycheck estimate because they're already in your pocket.

  • Overtime you can see coming

    A meter shows how close you are to overtime this workweek: over 40 hours at 1.5× by default (the US federal rule), adjustable or off for each job.

  • All your jobs

    Each job has its own rate, color, overtime rule and pay schedule: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly. Clock in to the right one with a tap.

  • Records for tax time

    A Year summary of wages, overtime premium, card tips, cash tips and tip-outs. Share it, or export every shift as a CSV file through your phone's share sheet.

  • Private by design

    No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Only you can see your shifts, jobs and paychecks, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

Overtime, paycheck and year figures are estimates based on what you enter. Some states and employers count overtime differently, and your pay stub is the official record. Nothing in ShiftTally is tax advice.
