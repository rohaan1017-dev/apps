Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

ShiftTally ("the app") is provided by Rohaan Patel ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

What you give the app so it can work, plus the sign-in records our authentication provider keeps for security:

DataExamplesWhy
Email address you@example.com Your account login, confirmation and password-reset emails
Password — Signing in. Stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider; we can't read it
Account ID A random identifier Links your shifts, jobs and paychecks to your account
Jobs Job name, colour, hourly rate, overtime rule, threshold and multiplier, the day your workweek starts, pay frequency and the first day of a pay period, whether the job is archived Working out overtime and paycheck estimates for each job
Shift records The job, start and end times, break minutes, hourly rate, card tips, cash tips, tip-outs, optional notes, when each record was created or changed Showing your shifts, hours, tips and pay
Paychecks you record The job and pay period, the gross amount you were paid, an optional note Comparing what you were paid with your own record ("Check my paycheck")
Sign-in and security records IP address, device or browser type (user agent), times of sign-ins, sign-outs, password resets and account changes Keeping your account secure (for example spotting unusual sign-ins) and running the service

The sign-in and security records are linked to your account. We don't use your IP address to work out where you are.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or bank or payment card details. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

Overtime, paycheck and year totals are worked out from this data. They're estimates for your own records, not payroll, legal or tax advice.

Exporting and sharing

Export all shifts (a CSV file) and Share summary (a short text of your year's totals) are created on your device and open your phone's share sheet (on the web, the file downloads). Nothing is sent to us or anyone else unless you choose where it goes, for example an email, a cloud drive or another app. Once you share it, the app or person you send it to handles it under their own terms. The app doesn't need any extra permission for this.

Where it's stored

Your account, jobs, shift records and paychecks are stored with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), which hosts our database and authentication on our behalf, in the AWS us-east-2 (Ohio, USA) region. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Database access rules make sure each account can only read and change its own shifts, jobs and paychecks.

The app keeps your sign-in session on your device so you stay signed in. It's removed when you sign out or delete your account.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. If you delete your account, your account and all your jobs, shift records and paychecks are deleted immediately from the live database. Copies may remain in our provider's encrypted backups for a limited time before they're overwritten on the provider's backup schedule, and our authentication provider's security logs (sign-in events, IP address) are kept for a limited time by that provider before they're removed.

Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Profile tab → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. This deletes your account and every shift, job and paycheck straight away.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on Delete your account, or email rohaan1017@icloud.com from the address you signed up with, and we'll delete your account within [30] days.

You can also edit or delete individual shifts, edit or remove a recorded paycheck, and edit or archive a job at any time in the app. To keep a copy of your shifts first, use Export all shifts on the Profile tab.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us at rohaan1017@icloud.com to make a request. We process your data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract).

Children

ShiftTally is meant for adults (18 and over) and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
